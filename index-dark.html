How it works

From sign-up to prize money.

  1. Make your accountSign up with your phone number or Google and pick a username. It takes about a minute.
  2. Join a tournamentTap Join before registration closes. You are put in a game room, and the room ID and password come to your notifications.
  3. Play and get paidThe best players in each room go through to the final. Prizes are paid into your wallet and you can withdraw to mobile money.
