About Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile is a free-to-play shooter for your phone. Matches are quick and full of action, from team-versus-team multiplayer to the big battle royale.

On Countrex you play in organised tournaments. Each one has a countdown, an entry fee and a prize, and your wins move you up the rankings.

Fair play

Each tournament also has its own rules on its card.

  • Play on the latest version of the game and keep your game name the same as your Countrex profile.
  • Every player must use a normal, unmodified app. Cheat tools, mods and bots mean disqualification.
  • Be in the lobby on time. A player who is 10 minutes late loses the match.
  • Screenshot the final score and send it to the admins if there is a dispute.
  • Be respectful in chat. Insults and threats can get you banned.
