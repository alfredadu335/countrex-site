Help & Support

Quick answers to common questions.

Getting started

How do I create an account?

Tap Sign up, enter your phone number and the code we text you, then choose a username and a strong password.

I did not get my code.

Wait a minute, then tap Resend code. Check that your number is right. If it still does not arrive, tap Change number and try again, or contact support.

Can I change my username or country?

Yes. Open Settings → Account. Usernames are 3 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores and must be unique.

Tournaments

How do I join a tournament?

Open a game from the home page, pick a tournament that is open and tap Join tournament.

When does registration close?

Every tournament shows a countdown. When it reaches zero, registration is closed.

I got disconnected during a match.

Tell the admins straight away and send a screenshot. The rules on the tournament card decide what happens next.

Wallet

How do I add money?

Open Wallet, tap Add money, choose an amount and confirm.

How do I withdraw?

Open Wallet and tap Withdraw. For your safety you need a confirmed email (Settings → Security) and a payout account (Settings → Wallet & payouts).

Account and safety

I forgot my password.

Contact support and we will help you get back into your account.

How do I report cheating?

Contact support with the match, the other player’s username and a screenshot.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and tap Delete account. Withdraw any money in your wallet first.
