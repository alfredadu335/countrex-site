About EA SPORTS FC Mobile

EA SPORTS FC Mobile is a free-to-play football game for your phone. You build a squad of players, then take on other players in head-to-head matches.

On Countrex you play in organised tournaments. Each one has a countdown, an entry fee and a prize, and your wins move you up the rankings.

How it works

From sign-up to prize in three steps.

  1. Pick a tournamentChoose one with a countdown that suits you.
  2. Pay the entry from your walletTop up your wallet first if you need to.
  3. Play, report and winWin your matches to climb the rankings and take the prize.

Fair play

Each tournament also has its own rules on its card.

  • Play on the mobile version of the game and keep your in-game name the same as your Countrex profile.
  • Use the default match settings the admins announce. Do not change match length or difficulty.
  • If you disconnect early, the match is replayed. Quitting while behind is a forfeit.
  • Screenshot the full-time score and send it to the admins if there is a dispute.
  • Be respectful to your opponent before, during and after the match.
